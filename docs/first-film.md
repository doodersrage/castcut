# Your first film

About ten minutes, once ComfyUI and the models are in. Open **Film** (`/play`).

!!! tip "Just want to see it work?"
    **Make a starter film** creates a Cast lead, opens Day with the stills already queueing,
    and lands on Cut. If ComfyUI is offline, **Use demo stills** lets you practise the cut.

## 1. Create a character

On **Film**, give her a name and either:

- **From photo** — upload a picture of the face you want, or
- **Traits** — sex, age, ethnicity, height, body, hair.

**Create & continue** makes her **plate**: the full-body picture every still starts from.

## 2. Dress her *(optional)*

**Outfit** → **Choose…** clothing:

| Pick | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A **kit** | One of the built-in outfits |
| **Your photo** | Someone wearing it, or the clothes on a plain background |
| **Shoes** | A shoe kit, your own photo, or words |

Queue a try-on and **Keep** the one you like. It becomes the dressed plate for every still.

## 3. Plan the day

**Day** shows four slots, morning to night.

1. Pick a **Mood** in the plan bar — Everyday, Sport, Vacation, Suggestive…
2. **Suggest day** writes a place and a moment for each slot. Edit any of them.
3. **Queue day** renders the stills (about 20–60 s each).

!!! note
    Tap a card to open its **slot sheet**: the place, the moment, the pose, the clothes,
    a **line** for her to say, and **narration** for the film.

## 4. Animate

**Animate all** turns each still into a short clip. A slot with a **line** becomes a
talking clip, lip-synced, in her voice.

## 5. Cut

**Cut film** puts the clips together. Under **Cut options**:

- **Shape** — widescreen, vertical (Reels / Shorts) or square
- **Music** — upload a track, or **Score this film**
- **Shot list** — reorder, trim, frame, leave a shot out

The film lands in the Gallery and on the Cast page.
