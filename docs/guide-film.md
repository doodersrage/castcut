# Cut a film

**Cut film** on Day or Story joins the shots into one video. It saves to the Gallery and to
the Cast as an episode.

## Before it cuts

Cut checks every shot first (*Checking the shots…*): missing, black, a talking clip with no
sound, or a still that missed its pose or face. Then choose **Retry them first**,
**Leave them out** or **Cut anyway**.

## Cut options

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Shape** | Widescreen 16:9 · Vertical 9:16 · Square 1:1 |
| **Size** | 720p · 1080p |
| **Length** | As the shots hold · Fit to the music · 15 / 30 / 60 s |
| **Music** | Upload, paste a link, or **Score this film** (an original track) |
| **Levels** | Music and Lines — the music ducks under speech |
| **Look** | Crossfade · Slow zoom on stills · Title & captions |

## Shot list

Reorder · leave out · edit captions · hold a still longer · **frame** a still (waist up,
chest up) · **trim** a clip · pick the **poster**.

## Sound

| Sound | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Lines** | Talking clips — the Cast's voice |
| **Narration** | Each slot's Narration, recorded when the film is cut |
| **Room sound** | Under talking clips, and **Add sound** on silent ones |
| **Music** | Your track or **Score this film** |

## After

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Cast → Films** | Watch, see what went into it, **Re-cut** at another shape or size |
| **Cast → Seasons** | **Stitch season** joins the episodes |
| **Export for an editor** | A zip with FCPXML, EDL, subtitles and the clips |
| **Review link** | Share the film for comments |
