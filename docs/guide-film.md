# Cut a film

Cut has no page of its own — it lives where the shots are.

| To… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| **Cut the film**, set **Cut options** | **Day** → the *Ready to cut* section at the top of the board (it appears once a still has finished), or the **Cut film** button by the reel. On **Story**, *Cut film* under the reel. |
| **Re-cut, export, share** a finished film | **Cast page → Film & media → Films** → the buttons under the film |
| **Stitch a season** | **Cast page → Film & media → Seasons** |

The film saves to the Gallery and to the Cast as an episode.

## Before it cuts

Cut checks every shot first (*Checking the shots…*): missing, black, a talking clip with no
sound, or a still that missed its pose or face. Then choose **Retry them first**,
**Leave them out** or **Cut anyway**.

## Cut options

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Shape** | Widescreen 16:9 · Vertical 9:16 · Square 1:1 |
| **Size** | 720p · 1080p |
| **Length** | As the shots hold · Fit to the music · 15 / 30 / 60 s |
| **Music** | Upload, paste a link, or **Score this film** (an original track) |
| **Levels** | Music and Lines — the music ducks under speech |
| **Look** | Crossfade · Slow zoom on stills · Title & captions |

## Shot list

Reorder · leave out · edit captions · hold a still longer · **frame** a still (waist up,
chest up) · **trim** a clip · pick the **poster**.

## Sound

| Sound | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Lines** | Talking clips — the Cast's voice |
| **Narration** | Each slot's Narration, recorded when the film is cut |
| **Room sound** | Under talking clips, and **Add sound** on silent ones |
| **Music** | Your track or **Score this film** |

## After the cut

On the **Cast page → Film & media → Films**, under each film:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Re-cut** | The same film at another shape (vertical, square, widescreen) or size |
| **Export for an editor** | A zip with FCPXML (Resolve, Final Cut), an EDL, subtitles and the clips |
| **Share for review** | A read-only link that expires — anyone can watch it without an account |

!!! note
    These need a film cut in **2.6 or later** (it keeps the recipe). For an older film, cut
    the Day again.
