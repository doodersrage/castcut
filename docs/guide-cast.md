# Cast

A **Cast** member is a character that stays the same person in every picture.
Open one from **Cast** (`/characters`) or the Film page.

**Cast** shows everyone as portrait cards — the plate fills the card, with a ▶ badge for their
films and **On Film** on the one your film follows. Click a card to open their page; **Start a
film** and **⋯** (try on outfits, open Day, details, remove) are under it. **Start a film with…**,
**New character** and **Import** sit in the header, and the last card is **+ New character**.
Past four people, search by name and sort by name or recently changed.

A Cast's page starts with their profile: the plate large (with none, the frame is **Add a plate**),
a line of facts (*no plate · 1 look · 0 films*), their looks to switch between, their voice
(**Add a voice** / **Change** opens Bible → Voice), what's next (*2 of 7 done · Next: Plate ready*,
with that step's button; click the line for all seven), and **Start a film** · **or open in Outfit ·
Day · Scene · Story** · **More**. The tabs stay in reach as you scroll; **Bible** has a row to jump to
each of its sections; **Film & media** opens on the newest film, playing, with the others as
posters to pick.

## Film style and mood board

In the **Bible** tab, beside the Story rating:

- **Film style** — the look of every still this Cast's films render, on Day, Scene and Story: a
  ready style (35 mm film, muted teal and amber, soft pastel, moody, vivid summer, black and white)
  and / or your own words ("a 90s camcorder"). Off by default; it adds a style line after the ones
  that keep her face and clothes.
- **Mood board** — pictures pinned from this Cast's Gallery, each with a note: the light, places
  and colours the films aim for. **Mood** beside Day's, Scene's and Story's header shows it while you
  plan. Reference only — it changes no render.

## Character sheet

**Character sheet** (beside the Cast's actions) opens every look's plate with its outfit and
description, the voice, and her props (how each of her things looks), on one page to print or save
as PDF.

**Expression sheet** renders the plate as six feelings — neutral, smiling, laughing, sad, angry,
surprised — on Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo (about three minutes for all six, kept in the Gallery), then
opens them on one page: a consistency reference, and a check that the face holds as it moves.

## What a Cast keeps

| Part | What it does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plate** | The full-body picture every still starts from — the face comes from here | Cast page → Looks |
| **Looks** | Several plates for one person (hair, era, style); each has its own outfit | Cast page → Looks |
| **Traits** | Sex, age, ethnicity, height, body, hair — used in every prompt | Bible → Appearance |
| **Marks** | Tattoos, freckles, glasses… kept wherever they would show | Bible → Marks |
| **Props** | Her phone, bag, car, dog — described once, the same every time | Bible → Props |
| **Voice** | How she sounds in talking clips | Bible → Voice |
| **Places** | Her home and workplace, the same rooms in every still | Bible → Places |

!!! tip "Marks need the plate"
    A tattoo or pair of glasses in words alone rarely shows. Put it on the plate
    (**Edit plate**) and it carries into every still.

## Voice

1. **Audition voices** — four short clips of her saying hello in different voices.
2. Keep one, or **Use my own recording** (about 5 s of one person talking).
3. Nudge it **Deeper** or **Higher**.

Talking clips are then steered toward that voice.

**Accent** sets how they speak — American, Southern American, New York, Canadian, British, London,
Scottish, Irish, Welsh, Australian, New Zealand, South African, Indian, Jamaican, French, Spanish,
Mexican, Italian, German, Russian or Brazilian. Auditions are asked for it ("…in a warm, low voice,
with a Scottish accent"), so **Hear other voices** after picking one to keep a voice that has it;
Day and Story talking clips are told it too. A dubbed line keeps the language's own accent.

## Places

Every Cast has one **home** and one **workplace**. Any still set in one of their rooms
(bedroom, kitchen, living room, bathroom, hallway, balcony; desk, meeting room, office
kitchen) uses that place's look, so the rooms match from slot to slot.

- Pick another **design**, write **your own words**, or turn it **off**.
- **Photos of their rooms** — upload a real photo of a living room, kitchen, bedroom,
  bathroom or office. Stills set there are built around it:

| Still | Uses the photo? |
| --- | --- |
| Clothed, one person, Edit 2511 | Yes — furniture, colours, walls, windows |
| Clothed, one person, Rapid | On its short scene recipe, yes; with an Outfit Keep plate's long brief, the place words instead |
| Clothed, two people (a Cast partner), Edit 2511 | Yes — the photo takes the pose map's place |
| Clothed, three people (a partner and a friend), Edit 2511 or Qwen-Image 2.1 | Yes — as a fourth picture, after the three faces |
| Intimate, one person or two, Rapid | Yes (one person: the sofa and plants more than the whole room; two people with the long two-skeleton brief: as a fourth picture) |
| Clothed, one person, Qwen-Image 2.1 | Yes — furniture, colours, walls, windows |
| Clothed two people on Rapid or Qwen-Image 2.1, Klein | No — the place words instead |

## Move a Cast

**Export Cast file** keeps every look, outfit, wardrobe still and reference sheet.
**Import** it on another machine.

## Films and seasons

Each film you cut is an **episode** of the Cast's current season. On the Cast page's **Film & media** tab:

- **Film & media → Films** — watch, see what went into it, and under each film **Re-cut**, **Export for an editor** and **Share for review**.
- **Seasons** — **Stitch season** joins the episodes; **Open this Day again** brings a Day back.
