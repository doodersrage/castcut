# Cast

A **Cast** member is a character that stays the same person in every picture.
Open one from **Cast** (`/characters`) or the Film page.

## What a Cast keeps

| Part | What it does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plate** | The full-body picture every still starts from — the face comes from here | Cast page → Looks |
| **Looks** | Several plates for one person (hair, era, style); each has its own outfit | Cast page → Looks |
| **Traits** | Sex, age, ethnicity, height, body, hair — used in every prompt | Bible → Appearance |
| **Marks** | Tattoos, freckles, glasses… kept wherever they would show | Bible → Marks |
| **Props** | Her phone, bag, car, dog — described once, the same every time | Bible → Props |
| **Voice** | How she sounds in talking clips | Bible → Voice |
| **Places** | Her home and workplace, the same rooms in every still | Bible → Places |

!!! tip "Marks need the plate"
    A tattoo or pair of glasses in words alone rarely shows. Put it on the plate
    (**Edit plate**) and it carries into every still.

## Voice

1. **Audition voices** — four short clips of her saying hello in different voices.
2. Keep one, or **Use my own recording** (about 5 s of one person talking).
3. Nudge it **Deeper** or **Higher**.

Talking clips are then steered toward that voice.

## Places

Every Cast has one **home** and one **workplace**. Any still set in one of their rooms
(bedroom, kitchen, living room, bathroom, hallway, balcony; desk, meeting room, office
kitchen) uses that place's look, so the rooms match from slot to slot.

- Pick another **design**, write **your own words**, or turn it **off**.
- **Photos of their rooms** — upload a real photo of a living room, kitchen, bedroom,
  bathroom or office. Stills set there are built around it:

| Still | Uses the photo? |
| --- | --- |
| Clothed, one person, Edit 2511 | Yes — furniture, colours, walls |
| Intimate / Raunchy, one person, Rapid | Yes |
| Clothed on Rapid, or two people | No — the place words instead |

## Move a Cast

**Export Cast file** keeps every look, outfit, wardrobe still and reference sheet.
**Import** it on another machine.

## Films and seasons

Each film you cut is an **episode** of the Cast's current season. On the Cast page's **Film & media** tab:

- **Film & media → Films** — watch, see what went into it, and under each film **Re-cut**, **Export for an editor** and **Share for review**.
- **Seasons** — **Stitch season** joins the episodes; **Open this Day again** brings a Day back.
