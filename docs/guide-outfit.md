# Outfit

Dress the Cast once; every clothed still on Day and Story starts from that **dressed plate**.
Open **Outfit** (`/fitting`).

The page: who's trying on (and their plate) on the left, the try-on in the middle with
**Quality** under it, the clothes on the right, and a bar kept at the bottom with what's on
(**Plate ready · Cropped sage slip dress**) and **Try it on** · **Skip kit** · **Try it on, then
next kit**. With no plate yet, the middle asks for one first — **Upload plate** or **From
Gallery** — and the rest waits, faded.

## Pick the clothes

**Choose…** opens the clothing sheet (the same one on Day and Story):

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Kits** | Built-in outfits by type — full outfits, tops, bottoms, outerwear, formal, swimwear… |
| **Your photo** | **Worn photo** (someone wearing it — the clothes are cut out) or a **packshot** (clothes on a plain background) |
| **Footwear** | A shoe kit, **My shoes** (your photo), **In words**, Auto or Barefoot |

**Save for later** keeps your own clothes and shoes for any session.

Above the kits: the clothing types as chips (**All**, **Full outfits**, **Tops**, **Bottoms**,
**Outerwear**, **Swimwear**, **Formal & dressy**, **Sleepwear & robes**, **Traditional & cultural**
— the others are in the type list), **☆ Star this kit** for the one you're on, and a row of the
kits you starred or tried last, one click to put one on.

### Footwear editor

**Edit shoes** — beside **Browse** when a catalog pair is worn, or with **Save for later** on your
own shoe photo — opens the **footwear editor**:

- **Change it** — say what to change ("make them red", "a lower heel", "white soles") and
  **Change**: the picture is changed on Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo (about half a minute); **Back to the
  previous picture** undoes it.
- **Replace with a photo** — your own picture of the pair.
- **What the model is told** — the words stills read; say the change here too.

**Save and wear** wears the edited pair and keeps it in **My shoes**. A catalog pair is saved as your
own copy (the catalog keeps its own); a saved pair is changed in place.

## Try it on

1. Pick a **Quality**: **Fast** (front only), **Balanced** (front and back), **Best** (also checks each try-on).
2. **Queue** a try-on.
3. **Keep** the one you like — it becomes the dressed plate. **Pass** drops one.
4. **Before / after** under a try-on puts the plate and the try-on under one handle — drag it to
   see exactly what changed.

Keys (not while typing): **← →** the kit before / after, **Enter** tries it on, **N** tries it on
then moves to the next kit, **K** keeps the try-on on the stage.

!!! tip "Best checks the try-on for you"
    Each try-on gets a face match against the plate and a read of the outfit and hands:
    *Face 64% · Outfit 4/5*. The cleanest one is marked **Best match**.

## Pose

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **As the plate** | Keeps the plate's stance |
| **Custom pose** | The [pose editor](guide-day.md#the-pose-editor) — a 3D body: drag hands and feet, turn the head, orbit the camera; start from a named pose |
| **From a photo** | Reads the pose of someone in a photo |

The box *The prompt will open with* shows the pose in words. The models follow the words
more than the drawing, so check it before you queue.

A custom pose whose camera is **Waist up** or **Head & shoulders** is cut from the try-on around
her face on the Qwen-Image 2.1 engines (as on Day); the back view stays full length. A camera
closer than full body leaves her feet out of frame, so no shoe pass runs.

## Then

**Continue to Day**. Changing the plate, clothes, shoes or engine later dresses her again
(about a minute, once).
