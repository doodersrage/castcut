# On your phone

**Mobile Studio** is the same film loop, built for a phone. Open `/m` on the phone (same
address as the desk, with `/m` on the end).

| Tab | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Film** | Pick or start a Cast lead, resume where you were |
| **Day** | Plan, queue, animate, cut |
| **Outfit** | Clothing, shoes, try-ons |
| **Story** | Scenes, stills, clips, cut |
| **More** | Cast, Gallery, Queue, Settings |

!!! tip "Add to Home Screen"
    Your browser's **Add to Home Screen** gives it its own icon and a full-screen window.

Everything you make on the phone is on the desk too (with server storage on).
